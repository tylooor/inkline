Inkline — iPad note & drawing app design board

One-page visual design: hybrid notebook + whiteboard canvas with embedded cards, minimal two-element chrome, and per-document theming.

Vision. One canvas that bends to the way you think. Notebook pages when you want structure, an infinite whiteboard when you don't — and any page, clip, PDF, or media can live inside the whiteboard as a movable card (the Heptabase / AFFiNE model, but ink-first).

Personal use first; designed to scale to students, artists, and professionals. Local-only storage at launch — no accounts, no cloud dependency. Working name: Inkline.

Three modes, one document model

Every document is a canvas. A "notebook" is a canvas constrained to page boundaries; a "whiteboard" is the same surface unbounded; a "card" is a region of one document embedded inside another.

Minimal chrome — two controls, everything else is gesture

The whole UI is two floating elements over the canvas. Any tool is reachable in at most two taps.

The ink bar

One floating pill — pen, marker, pencil, eraser, lasso, insert. Draggable to any edge or corner; remembers its position per document. Tap a tool to select it; tap it again for its options (color, width, nib). Two taps to anything.

The nav element

One rounded-corner element in a fixed corner: back to library, document title, and a context menu (theme, export, delete). No tab bars, no side panels, no persistent chrome competing with the ink.

Per-document theming

Theme is a property of the document, not the app. Switching a note from paper to dark mode remaps ink colors automatically.

Light paper

Cream texture, ruled or grid, blue-black inks. The GoodNotes feel.

Dark

True-black page, inks auto-invert to light equivalents. Easy on eyes, great for sketching glow.

Clean digital

White or dotted canvas, no texture — the whiteboard's native look.

Scope & sequencing

Phase 1 — feels complete

Ink engine (pen, marker, pencil, eraser, lasso), notebook + whiteboard modes, embedded cards, per-document theming, library, undo/redo, local storage.

Phase 2 — the power features

Shape assist (ink → clean shapes), PDF import & annotation, image & audio cards, layers, PDF/image export.

Later — reach

Handwriting search (OCR), iCloud sync, sharing/collaboration, custom templates, iPhone companion view.

Open decisions

Embedded cards: live link or snapshot? A card that mirrors its source page stays current but couples documents; a snapshot is safe but stale. Proposal: live link by default, "freeze" as a card action.

Whiteboard rendering. PencilKit canvases are large but finite — the whiteboard tiles PKCanvasView regions around content rather than faking infinity with a fixed bounds. Performance-tested early.

Simulator vs. device. Pressure, tilt, hover, and double-tap can't be exercised in the iOS simulator — feel and palm rejection need on-device iteration early, not at the end.

Getting it on your iPad. Build & sign on your Mac: free Apple ID works (7-day signing), Developer Program unlocks year-long installs and TestFlight for a wider beta.
